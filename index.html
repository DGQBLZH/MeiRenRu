About Us

Crafting fan lights for over a decade.

Zhongshan Meirenru Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. evolved from Hong Kong Meirenru Lighting & Electrical—a large modern enterprise dedicated to developing, manufacturing, and selling luxury decorative fan lights.

Founded in Hong Kong twelve years ago, Meirenru has always put user needs first, focusing on quality, product innovation, and attentive after-sales service. In late 2021, we registered Zhongshan Meirenru Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. to better serve the domestic market. Today we occupy over 10,000 m², employ more than 200 team members, operate advanced international production lines, and maintain ISO9001 and RoHS compliance—with a defect rate below 0.03%.

Meirenru luxury decorative fan lights are recognized for outstanding quality, strong reputation, dedicated service, and deep R&D capabilities—consistently ranking among the industry's best. With integrity, quality, and service at our core, we create a healthier, higher-quality life for millions of homes.
