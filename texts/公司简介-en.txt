Zhongshan Merenro Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. was formerly known as Hong Kong Merenro Lighting Appliances, a large-scale modern enterprise specializing in the development, production, and sales of luxury decorative fan lights.

With its journey reflecting its dreams, Hong Kong Merenro Lighting Appliances was established 12 years ago in Hong Kong—a city renowned for its global business reputation. Over the past decade, Merenro has consistently focused on customer needs, prioritized quality, driven product innovation, and delivered dedicated after-sales service, earning the trust and acclaim of numerous customers. To better serve the domestic market, the company officially registered and established Zhongshan Merenro Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. at the end of 2021, aiming to deepen its presence and service within China. From Hong Kong Merenro Appliances to today's Merenro Lighting, the company has grown into a facility covering over 10,000 square meters, with a team of more than 200 professionals. Equipped with internationally advanced production equipment and a high-caliber research and development department, the company strictly adheres to ISO9001 quality management standards and fully complies with ROHS directives. With comprehensive improvements in production and team management, the product defect rate has been reduced to less than three per ten thousand units, setting new industry benchmarks.

Merenro luxury decorative fan lights have consistently ranked among the top in the industry thanks to their superior quality, strong user reputation, attentive after-sales service, and robust R&D capabilities. Embracing inclusiveness and excellence, Merenro luxury decorative fan lights are committed to building a high-quality, healthy lifestyle for millions of households through integrity, quality, and service.
